Why there are no 1H/4H trade setups here

We tested 1H and 4H setups (4H breakout on volume, wick rejection at daily support, RSI divergence) on 43 coins, only in daily uptrends, with stops 1 to 3.5% under the nearest swing low and targets at the nearest resistance. On 4H: breakouts won 1 of 3, wick reversals averaged 0%, divergence +0.7% on only 7 trades, and random entries under the same filters did about as well. On 1H every setup lost money. Tight stops get hit by normal noise, so about 3 in 4 trades stop out. Until a version beats random entries, showing them as setups would be guessing dressed up as precision.
